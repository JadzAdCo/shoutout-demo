VIP reserV Terms of use (beta)

These beta Terms apply to VIP reserV table holds and deposit payments on FLOQR. Live venue Terms will replace this placeholder when counsel publishes them. By accepting, you agree to the rules below for the club, night, and table you selected.

Reservation & hold

A green table hold lasts 30 minutes. Paying the deposit confirms the reservation for that night and section. Bars, DJ booths, and other non-bookable areas cannot be reserved.

Deposit, refund & cancellation

The amount shown as “Pay today” is charged now via FloqR Stripe Checkout. Refund and cancellation follow the venue’s published policy. Untimely cancellation or failure to arrive may forfeit the deposit in whole or in part.

No-show

If your party does not check in by the venue’s cutoff, the club may mark a no-show and keep the deposit. Opening a notify or Inbox link never changes reservation status by itself.

Age & ID

You confirm you are 21 or older and will present valid government ID at the door for every guest who requires it under local law or venue policy.

Minimum spend & gratuity

Table minimums, extra-guest fees, and gratuity are disclosed on the floor map before you hold. The deposit may apply toward the minimum; remaining balance is paid at the venue.

Privacy

FLOQR stores your consent with Terms version and hash for audit. Marketing messages from the venue are optional and separate from this required acceptance.
