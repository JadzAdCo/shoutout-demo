Who we are

FLOQR (also styled FloqR) operates nightlife discovery, ShoutOut LED messaging, Mingl, scheduling, and related venue tools. Production brand host: https://www.floqr.com. Until cutover, this demo may run on GitHub Pages.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, phone, display name, auth providers
  • Profile datapoints you choose to share (city, music, nightlife interests, media)
  • Transactional data: ShoutOuts, schedules, payments (Stripe), Inbox
  • Security signals: App Check / reCAPTCHA, optional client IP for display security and compliance logs
  • Device / browser signals needed to run the web app (and later hybrid native apps)

Why we process data

  • Provide the service you request (ShoutOut, Mingl, Work Calendar, Club Admin)
  • Security and abuse prevention (OTP, SOS2FA, App Check)
  • Optional marketing and analytics only when you consent in My Privacy
  • Legal retention for ShoutOut compliance logs (media ~90 days; metadata longer — see in-product retention help)

Processors we use

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, App Check / reCAPTCHA Enterprise)
  • Twilio (SMS / WhatsApp)
  • SendGrid (system email)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Google Places / Gemini where venue discovery or AI features are enabled

Do Not Sell or Share (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information for money. We may use profile tags for in-app ad relevance. You can opt out of sale/share-style personalization:

  • My Profile → My Privacy → turn off third-party sharing and/or enable Do Not Sell or Share
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control (GPC), we treat that as an opt-out signal and set Do Not Sell or Share

Opt-out stops personalized profile-tag ad scoring. House / non-targeted creatives may still appear.

Your rights

  • Access / portability: My Privacy → Download My Data (server export when signed in)
  • Delete: My Privacy → Request Data Delete (fulfilled by FLOQR; not ticket-only after this release)
  • Correct: edit My Profile and My Privacy
  • EU/UK and other regions: contact the controller listed at cutover for additional rights requests

Cookies and Consent Mode

Strictly necessary security (reCAPTCHA / App Check) may run so you can sign in. Marketing and analytics cookies/SDKs are not enabled until Consent Mode v2 defaults and a consent UI are live. A dormant Google Analytics measurement ID in config must not be activated without those controls.

Hybrid apps and other countries

The same preference fields and DSAR callables apply when FLOQR ships as a hybrid native app. Regional notices (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, etc.) will use the same privacy core with localized copy.

Contact

Until cutover completes, demo privacy requests: use My Privacy in the app or email the Master Admin contact on file for this Firebase project. Production contact will be published on https://www.floqr.com/privacy.html.
